## A note from Bob:

It's been a minute. And while there are just a few new blog posts in this issue, there are a bunch of new releases, and a ton of new sites.

## Highlights

**Pre-release me now.** Zach has been working hard resolving and responding to [GitHub issues on the 11ty repo](https://github.com/11ty/buildawesome/issues). As I'm writing this, there are only 15 open issues, which is quite remarkable. I've been watching the increased activity, expecting a new release. Zach did not disappoint, with the  Pre-release of Build Awesome (11ty) v4.0.0-alpha.11. The [release notes](https://github.com/11ty/buildawesome/releases/tag/v4.0.0-alpha.11) are extensive with a few breaking changes highlighted. Check it out and report any issues you find.

**Start here.** [Adam DJ Brett](https://www.adamdjbrett.com) put together [an excellent guide](https://www.adamdjbrett.com/blog/2026-11-16-getting-started-11ty-build-awesome/) for how to get started with 11ty/Build Awesome. It's filled with practical setup instructions and deployment options.

**Show me the PDF.** [Raymond Camden](https://www.raymondcamden.com), our most prolific [author](https://11tybundle.dev/authors/raymond-camden/), shows [how to embed PDFs in Eleventy posts](https://www.raymondcamden.com/2026/10/21/adding-a-pdf-display-to-eleventy) using a shortcode.
