## A note from Bob:

The first 3 highlights below are from the posts in this issue. That said, I've got a bunch of other stuff that hit my radar over the last couple of months when I had lapsed in generating new issues. So I've added them to the highlights section.

Note that this is the third in a 3-part series of me catching up on what I've captured over the last few months.

---

## Highlights

**CodePens on the blog.** [Raymond Camden](https://www.raymondcamden.com), in his 107th post, shows us how he [Adds CodePens to his Blog](https://www.raymondcamden.com/2026/10/06/adding-my-latest-codepens-to-my-blog).

**Hosting her own podcasts.** [Veronica Explains](https://vkc.sh/), entertaining as always, shows how she hosts her own podcasts in [Most podcasting platforms suck, so I host my own.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53kc2QjGxnY).

**Build a modern blog.** [Richard Djarbeng](https://rdjarbeng.com) brings us [Building a Modern Blog with Eleventy 3.x (Liquid Edition)](https://rdjarbeng.com/building-a-modern-blog-with-eleventy-3-x-liquid-edition/), a comprehensive guide to building a functional blog using Eleventy. This post is very thorough and if you're on the fence about blogging with Eleventy, it's well worth a read.

Other items of note...

**New leaderboard home.** The Eleventy Leaderboards have a new home at [speedlify2](https://www.speedlify.dev/). Zach says _"I’m delighted to show off some huge updates and improvements have been made to the brand new Speedlify2."_ Check out the blog post about it at [Use Speedlify2 to Continuously Measure Website Performance—zachleat.com](https://www.zachleat.com/web/speedlify2/).

**Rangefinding.** Many of us have integrated [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app/) into our sites to provide our visitors with fast, client-side search capabilities. There's a new kid on the block called Rangefind. Learn more about it at [Rangefind — Search, without the server.](https://rangefind.dev/).

**All-in-one.** If you're looking for an _"All-in-One kit for 11ty / Build Awesome,"_ you can find it at [11ty / Build Awesome One](https://buildawesome.one/).

**EPUB for 11ty.** If you've got docs that you want to convert to an EPUB, [Katie Ampersand](https://codeberg.org/LilithInStarlight) has you covered with [LilithInStarlight/3pub: An Eleventy template for making EPUBs.](https://codeberg.org/LilithInStarlight/3pub).

**Mastodon share.** [Kyle Reddoch](https://www.kylereddoch.me/) has developed a _"simple Eleventy plugin that helps readers share your posts to Mastodon without forcing them to manually edit instance URLs."_ Check it out at [kylereddoch/eleventy-plugin-mastodon-share](https://github.com/kylereddoch/eleventy-plugin-mastodon-share#eleventy-plugin-mastodon-share).

**Begin at the beginning.** [Brennan Kenneth Brown](https://brennan.day/) brings a useful resource for beginners looking to get started with static site generators. Read it at [Static Site Generators for Beginners](https://brennan.day/static-site-generators-for-beginners/).

**Tumble into Eleventy.** If you've got a site or blog on Tumblr (remember [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/)?)and have considered migrating it to Eleventy, give [Tool for migrating Tumblr blog posts to an Eleventy static site.](https://github.com/corbindavenport/tumblr-to-eleventy) a try.

**Exploring Eleventy.** While the initial version of this, found here in the [Showcase](https://11tybundle.dev/showcase/eleventy-explorer-netlify-app/), was built with Eleventy, this new one is not. But it's still pretty cool. Check it out at [Eleventy Explorer v2.3.3](https://eleventy-explorer.tigersway.dev/).

**Getting comical.** Here's a short video showing how [Gary Beason](https://www.youtube.com/@GaryBeason) creates new webcomics each week with Eleventy:
[Webcomic Test & Publish Walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1h2qfnftiKQ)
